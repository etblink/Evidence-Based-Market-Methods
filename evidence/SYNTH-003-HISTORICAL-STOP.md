# SYNTH-003 — Historical Research Stop Decision

Captured: 2026-10-02

Purpose: decide whether additional retrospective market-method research has enough expected decision value to justify more of the project owner's finite time.

Authority boundary: this file records synthesis. `STATE.yaml` remains the sole canonical current state.

## What survived

### Diversified trend / time-series momentum

This is the strongest **accessible surviving candidate** from the completed historical program.

Evidence chain:

1. Broad literature and long-history evidence justified retention during reconnaissance.
2. AUDIT-004 Stage A used the directly downloaded official AQR aggregate TSMOM factor, without asset-class selection or parameter changes.
3. Under the rule frozen before downloading the workbook, Stage A was a `clear_survivor`:
   - 2013-01 through 2026-05: +5.37% annualized arithmetic mean, 0.41 Sharpe, +83.36% compounded, -27.91% maximum drawdown.
   - 2020-01 through 2026-05: +4.22% annualized arithmetic mean, 0.32 Sharpe, +23.90% compounded, -24.56% maximum drawdown.
4. AUDIT-004 Stage B then tested an accessible transparent implementation using official provider data only.
5. KMLM passed the predeclared implementation rule:
   - 3-year NAV annualized: +0.41%.
   - 5-year NAV annualized: +5.94%.
   - since-inception NAV annualized: +8.23%.
   - expense ratio: 0.90%.
   - corresponding KFA MLM Index annualized returns: 1.51%, 7.98%, and 10.17%.
6. The product is not the AQR factor. KMLM uses a daily long/short signal based on price relative to a long-term moving average across 22 commodity, currency, and bond futures markets.

Interpretation:

- The existence/persistence case for diversified trend is materially stronger than for generic technical-analysis rule mining.
- A transparent individual implementation exists.
- Implementation is not frictionless; the historical index-to-NAV gap is material.
- Multi-year weakness is real: KMLM's current 3-year annualized NAV return is only +0.41%.
- This is evidence of a real candidate, not evidence that a particular person should allocate capital to it.

### Profitability / quality

Scientific signal: retained.

Implementation conclusion: **deferred**.

AUDIT-002 found a strong large-stock research-portfolio result. AUDIT-003 then showed why research-factor success cannot be promoted casually into an investable conclusion:

- the first common ETF data source failed an official-return integrity check;
- official 2020–2025 product returns placed JQUA modestly behind VTI and SPHQ only slightly ahead;
- the product-level advantage was therefore small, source-sensitive, and not worth escalating into expensive point-in-time security reconstruction.

### Cross-sectional momentum

Scientific phenomenon: retained.

Immediate individual long-only path: **not supported**.

The long-short Mom factor survived AUDIT-001, but the predeclared large-stock long-only momentum representation failed AUDIT-002 against the broad U.S. market in both the 2016+ and 2020+ windows.

### Value

Scientific phenomenon: retained.

Priority implementation path: **not supported by the project's frozen screen**.

HML was weak in the 2016+ audit, with negative compounded factor return and a maximum drawdown greater than 50%.

### Earnings surprise / revision underreaction

Scientific question: still serious.

Next test: **deferred**.

Recent literature remains mixed and a clean classic test generally requires expensive point-in-time analyst expectations data such as I/B/E/S. Public substitutes either change the hypothesis or impose substantial engineering cost.

## What was cheaply eliminated or deferred

The reconnaissance also prevented the project from spending major time on:

- classical chart-pattern/indicator rule mining;
- short-term and long-term reversal as first experiments;
- broad discretionary stock picking;
- merger arbitrage as a first individual method;
- generic directional macro forecasting;
- statistical arbitrage;
- options/variance-premium harvesting;
- microstructure/latency strategies;
- short-horizon news/sentiment processing.

Several of these contain real phenomena. They were deferred because the likely individual opportunity was not attractive enough relative to data, infrastructure, tail risk, complexity, or time cost.

## Epistemic status

No completed retrospective test is a pristine prospective confirmation.

The project has nevertheless done enough to answer the original reconnaissance question:

> Are there market methods with stronger evidence and practical accessibility than generic discretionary technical analysis?

**Yes.** Diversified trend following is the clearest surviving example in this program. Profitability/quality also has strong research evidence but did not earn further implementation work under the project's opportunity-cost constraint.

The remaining uncertainty about trend is now mostly **prospective** rather than something another historical parameter search can solve honestly.

## Stop decision

Additional retrospective strategy research is **not justified now**.

Reasons:

1. The project has already mapped the main method families.
2. Weak and inaccessible branches were eliminated cheaply.
3. The strongest accessible survivor has both an updated research factor and a live transparent implementation.
4. Another retrospective test would increasingly measure researcher flexibility rather than add independent evidence.
5. The owner's explicit objective includes protecting finite life-hours from becoming trapped in studying how to earn rather than earning.

The appropriate next scientific step, if ever desired, is a separately frozen prospective observation of trend or a separate capital-allocation/suitability analysis. Neither is automatically authorized by this historical program.

## Capital boundary

Nothing in this synthesis recommends buying KMLM, changing an existing portfolio, or deploying capital.

A capital decision would require a separate analysis of objectives, risk tolerance, portfolio role, alternatives, taxes, liquidity, drawdowns, sizing, and opportunity cost, followed by explicit human authorization.

## Program conclusion

**Active retrospective research stops here.**

Reopen only if:
- the owner explicitly chooses a prospective trend-validation program;
- the owner asks for a separate capital-allocation decision analysis;
- materially new evidence changes the relative ranking of deferred methods;
- a substantially cheaper clean test becomes available for a deferred high-quality candidate.
